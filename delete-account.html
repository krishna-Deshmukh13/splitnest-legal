Permanent Action Notice

Deleting your SplitNest account is permanent and irreversible. Your account credentials, private personal notes, and profile records will be wiped immediately.

Method 1: Direct In-App Deletion (Recommended)

If you have the SplitNest application installed on your Android device, you can delete your account instantly:

  1. Open the SplitNest app on your Android device.
  2. Tap the Profile tab in the bottom navigation bar (or your avatar icon in the top right).
  3. Scroll down to the bottom of the Profile screen to find "Delete Account" (highlighted in red).
  4. Tap Delete Account.
  5. An alert dialog will appear outlining the impact. Tap "Delete Permanently" to confirm.
  6. The app immediately deletes your profile and authentication record from Firebase, clears all locally stored SQLite Room database caches, and redirects to the Welcome screen.

Method 2: Web Deletion Request (If App is Uninstalled)

If you have uninstalled SplitNest or cannot access your device, you can submit an account deletion request via email:

  • Send an email to: krishnadeshmukh721@gmail.com
  • Subject: SplitNest Account Deletion Request
  • Body: Please include:
    • The email address associated with your SplitNest account (Google account or registration email).
    • A brief statement confirming you want your account and profile data permanently deleted.

Processing Time: Web deletion requests are processed manually within 7 business days. You will receive an email confirmation once your account records have been permanently removed.

What Data Is Deleted vs. Retained

In accordance with Google Play developer policies and financial ledger integrity requirements, here is the exact breakdown of data deletion:

Data That Is Permanently Deleted:

  • User Profile: Your display name, email, profile photo URL, UPI ID, and preferred currency.
  • Cloud Authentication: Your Firebase Authentication user account and access tokens.
  • Personal Notes & Checklists: All private text notes, checklists, and packing lists stored in users/{userId}/personal_notes.
  • Local Device Storage: The entire local SQLite database, biometric PIN hashes, and cached user preferences on your phone.

Data Retained for Group Ledger Mathematical Integrity:

  • Shared Group Transactions: If you participated in shared group expenses (e.g., you paid for a dinner or were allocated a portion of a trip), those historical line items remain in the group's ledger with your name replaced by an anonymized placeholder (e.g. "Former Member").
  • Why is this retained? Retaining the numerical values of past transactions is essential so that other real group members do not experience corrupted balances, broken math, or incorrect debts among themselves.

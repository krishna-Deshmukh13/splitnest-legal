⚠️ Irreversible Action Warning

Deleting your SplitNest account is permanent. Once completed, your user authentication credentials, cloud profile, personal notes, checklists, and local app cache cannot be recovered.

Your Right to Data Deletion

At SplitNest, we respect your privacy and provide full control over your personal information. In compliance with Google Play Store User Data and Account Deletion policies, you have two simple and accessible methods to permanently delete your account and associated personal records:

  • Method 1: Direct In-App Deletion (Immediate — complete in 10 seconds from within SplitNest).
  • Method 2: Web Deletion Request (Email-based — for users who have uninstalled the app or no longer have device access).
Method 1 (Instant)

Delete Directly Inside the SplitNest App

If you currently have SplitNest installed on your Android device, you can delete your account instantly without waiting for manual verification:

  1. Open the SplitNest app on your Android device.
  2. Tap the Profile tab in the bottom navigation bar (or your avatar icon in the top right corner).
  3. Scroll down to the bottom of the screen to the Account Actions section.
  4. Tap Delete Account (highlighted with a trash icon).
  5. A confirmation dialog will appear explaining the permanent nature of the deletion.
  6. Tap Delete Permanently to execute.

What happens automatically: The app invokes Firebase Authentication to delete your user identity, removes your profile document from Cloud Firestore (/users/{userId}), resets local DataStore preferences, and purges cached SQLite Room tables from your device. You are immediately returned to the Welcome screen.

Method 2 (Web / Uninstalled)

Submit a Web Account Deletion Request

If you have uninstalled SplitNest, lost access to your device, or prefer to request deletion via the web, you can submit an official deletion request directly to our developer team:

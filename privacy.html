Our Core Privacy Commitment

SplitNest is built to simplify shared expenses among friends, roommates, and travel groups. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information or financial records to data brokers or advertising networks. Receipt scanning and voice expense entry run 100% on your device, and sensitive screen thumbnails are shielded from unauthorized capture.

1. Introduction & Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how SplitNest ("we", "us", or "our"), developed by Krishna Deshmukh, collects, uses, stores, shares, and protects your information when you use our Android mobile application (Package ID: com.splitnest.expenses) and related synchronization services.

SplitNest is a peer-to-peer expense calculation and shared debt tracking application. It calculates transparent ledger balances, assists in recording repayments, and facilitates group discussions. SplitNest is not a bank, wallet, payment processor, or depository financial institution.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to authenticate your account, maintain collaborative group ledgers, and calculate accurate financial balances.

A. Information You Provide Directly

  • Account & Profile Information: When you register or sign in using Google Sign-In or email credentials, we receive your account identifier (UID), display name, email address, and profile photo URL. You may optionally supply a preferred base currency (e.g., INR, USD, EUR) and a UPI Virtual Payment Address (VPA) to expedite settlement link creation.
  • Financial Ledger & Expense Data: When you add or edit an expense, we store the title/description, amount in minor currency units (paise/cents), original currency code, expense category (e.g., Groceries, Food, Travel), timestamp, designated payers, and allocation shares among participants.
  • Settlements & Repayment Records: Information regarding repayments made between group members, including payer ID, recipient ID, repaid amount, payment method (e.g., UPI, Cash), and confirmation status.
  • Groups & Member Roster: Group names, group categories, cover avatars, member lists, and membership roles (Owner, Editor, Member).
  • Personal Notes & Interactive Checklists: Private text notes, packing checklists, and itinerary items you create for personal productivity.
  • In-App Discussions: Messages, timestamps, and sender identities posted in one-on-one direct messages or group discussions.

B. Information Collected Automatically

  • Device & Usage Data: Operating system version, device model, app lifecycle events, and crash diagnostic logs necessary for stability monitoring.
  • Advertising Identifiers (Free Tier Only): Google Advertising ID (GAID) collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK to display banner advertisements to non-subscribers.

3. Device Permissions & On-Device Processing

SplitNest requests only runtime permissions essential to optional, user-initiated features. We strictly enforce on-device processing to ensure your media and voice data remain private.

Android Permission Feature / Purpose Privacy & Processing Guarantee
android.permission.CAMERA Receipt Scanning (OCR) 100% On-Device Processing: Images captured with the camera are processed exclusively on your device using bundled Google ML Kit text recognition models. No photos or receipt text are ever transmitted to external cloud servers.
android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO Voice Expense Entry Ephemeral Speech Recognition: Audio is processed via your Android system's built-in SpeechRecognizer service. Audio recordings are never saved to files or uploaded to our servers.
android.permission.READ_CONTACTS Add Person from Contacts Single Contact Selection Only: Accessed only when you tap "Add from Contacts". Only the single contact you pick (name, phone/email) is imported into the expense. We never read, harvest, or upload your entire contact book.
android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS Alerts & Notifications Used to deliver bill payment reminders, group join approvals, and incoming message alerts. You can disable notifications anytime in Android Settings.

Biometric & PIN App Lock

SplitNest includes an App Lock feature utilizing a 4-to-6 digit salted SHA-256 PIN and AndroidX BiometricPrompt (Fingerprint / Face Unlock). Biometric templates are stored securely in your device's hardware Secure Enclave / Trusted Execution Environment (TEE). SplitNest never has access to or stores your biometric markers.

5. Data Sharing with Other Group Members

Because SplitNest is a collaborative application, certain information is inherently shared with peers you invite or connect with:

  • Roster Visibility: Members of a shared group can see your display name, profile avatar, and email address to verify who is sharing expenses.
  • Ledger Transparency: All members of a group can view expenses, payer amounts, individual split shares, and repayments recorded within that group.
  • Discussions: Messages sent in a group chat are visible to all members of that group.
  • Personal Data Isolation: Your personal notes, checklists, and separate groups are strictly private and cannot be viewed by members of other groups.

6. Data Security & Storage

We protect your financial information using multiple layers of technical security:

  • Encryption in Transit: All communications between your device and Firebase servers use TLS/HTTPS encryption.
  • Local Database Security: Data cached on your device is protected by Android application sandboxing.
  • Screen Capture & Recents Masking: When enabled in Profile Settings, SplitNest applies FLAG_SECURE to mask application balance thumbnails in the Android task switcher and prevent unauthorized screen captures.
  • Granular Authorization: Cloud Firestore Security Rules v2 strictly enforce that only authenticated members of a group can read or write expenses. Debtors are blocked from falsely confirming their own repayments.

7. Data Retention & Account Deletion

We retain your personal data for as long as your account remains active or as needed to provide you with group expense calculations.

How to Delete Your Account:

  1. In-App Deletion: Open SplitNest $\rightarrow$ navigate to Profile $\rightarrow$ tap Delete Account $\rightarrow$ confirm permanent deletion.
  2. Online Web Deletion Request: If you cannot access the app, visit our Account Deletion Information Page or email krishnadeshmukh721@gmail.com with the subject "SplitNest Account Deletion Request".

Data Deletion Impact:

  • Immediate Purge: Your user profile, email credentials, cloud authentication record, and private personal notes are permanently wiped.
  • Shared Ledger Preservation: To protect the mathematical accuracy of remaining group members who shared past expenses with you, historical transactions retain an anonymized placeholder (e.g., "Former Member") with no link to your personal contact details.

8. Your Rights & Privacy Choices

Depending on your jurisdiction (such as GDPR in Europe, CCPA in California, or the Digital Personal Data Protection Act in India), you have rights regarding your information:

  • Access & Portability: You can export your transaction history into CSV, Excel, or PDF formats directly from the app.
  • Correction: You can edit your display name, profile photo, and default currency at any time from the Profile screen.
  • Withdraw Consent: You can revoke device permissions (Camera, Microphone, Contacts, Notifications) via your Android system settings at any time.
  • Opt-Out of Personalized Ads: Free tier users can adjust personalized ad settings in their Android Device Settings under Google $\rightarrow$ Ads $\rightarrow$ Reset or Delete Advertising ID.

If we become aware that a child has provided us with personal information, we will delete it promptly.
